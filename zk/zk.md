ZKP - proof that allows one party to convince another party that they know something without revealing information.
1. Completeness (honest can confirm)
2. Soundness (dishonest can't confirm)
3. Zero-knowlegde

Type of proofs:
1. Interactive
	- multiple rounds to convince
2. Non-interactive
	- 1 round (prover <=> verifier)
	- types:

Terminology:
- claim/statement (is true)
- private and public inputs
- constraint - mathematical condition that must be satisfied
- circuit (system of constraint)
- witness
- prover
- verifier
- trusted setup

- proof of computation => proof of knowledge

1. Arithmetise a problem into circuit
2. Backend: generate + verify proof

- occurs to a liquidity provider
- good for LP provider (ratio remains): 
	- both assets stay the same price
	- both goes up
- bad for LP provider(ratios change):
	- one goes up/down
	- both move in different directions

k - product of token counts, not $
- so if one token price moves, ratio remains the same, but price drops

###### Avoid Impermanent Loss:
1. Provide in stablecoin pairs
2. Avoid risky/volatile coins
3. Provide when a coin price is low (loss, but total value goes up)
4. Provide to places that offer extra incentives 
5. Provide to a pool that isn't 50/50
6. Pools with more than 2 assets

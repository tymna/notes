## Rounding problem
- Solidity has no floats → rounds to 0
	- 9,8 => 9
- < 1 share worth → 0 shares
- rounding always favors the vault
- **inflation/donation attack**: attacker gets 1 share, donates assets to inflate exchange rate → next depositor rounds to 0 shares
	`shares = 9999 × 1 / 10001 = 0.9999... ~ 0`
	`shares = deposit * totalShares / totalAssets
- **fix**: virtual(don't exist) offsets (e.g. OZ adds +1 to shares and assets) so rate can't be cheaply manipulated
	` shares = deposit * (totalShares + 10**offset) / (totalAssets + 1)`
	- everyone gets more shares
		- slicing a pizza into 1000 pieces instead of 10.
		- hold more slices, but each slice is smaller
		- your portion of the pizza hasn't changed
	- attack becomes 1 million times more expensive,
	   making it economically pointless
	- offset = 6: attacker must donate ~10,000,000,000 USDC to steal 10,000 USDC — loses 10 billion to gain 10k

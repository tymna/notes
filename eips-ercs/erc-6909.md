simpler version or ERC1155:
	token ID -> balance
designed for: multiple internal token balances within one contract ([[singleton-design-uniswap-v4]])
- internal accounting
	- safe transfers, but only inside (balances on real ERC-20 differs)
```
PoolManager
   ↓
"you have 100 USDC"
"you have 2 ETH"
"you have 50 WBTC"
```

So our real ERC20 seats inside [[PoolManager-uniswap-v4]], but they are represented as 6909 copies of tokens for easier internal accounting. But actual ERC20 balances are updated at the end of the transfer/settle.

internal accounting: ERC-6909
final settlement: ERC20
